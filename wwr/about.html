History

Founded in 1999, White Water Rafting has been providing unforgettable experiences. In the misty dawn of civilization, the river's gentle voice whispered secrets to ancient ears.

Centuries passed, and the river's siren call beckoned explorers to uncharted shores. Francisco Pizarro and John Wesley Powell answered, their rafts bucking the rapids as they charted the unknown.
